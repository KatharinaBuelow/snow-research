# Ensemble Assessment & Statistical Robustness

Statistical significance of climate-change signals is assessed for each climate variable at each individual grid point and for each simulation.

- A change is defined as **significant** if it cannot be explained by random (natural) fluctuations with a probability of **less than 5%** ($p < 0.05$) according to a **Mann–Whitney U test**.

A scenario ensemble is classified as showing a **robust increase** if:

- at least **2/3** of the simulations show an increase, and
- at least **50%** of the simulations show a significant increase.

Analogously, a **robust decrease** is defined if:

- at least **2/3** of the simulations show a decrease, and
- at least **50%** of the simulations show a significant decrease.
